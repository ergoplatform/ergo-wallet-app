Official Ergo Wallet App - Your secure gateway to the Ergo blockchain

Features:
• Generate new wallets or restore existing wallets (compatible with Yoroi and Ergo node)
• Add read-only wallets to watch balances without exposing secrets
• Lightweight client - no full blockchain sync needed
• Request payments via QR code or shareable links
• Send payments manually or by scanning QR codes
• Display and send tokens and NFTs
• Password-encrypted or biometric-protected storage
• Multi-wallet support with configurable fiat currency comparison
• Cold wallet support for enhanced security
• ErgoPay protocol support
• ErgoAuth authentication support

Security & Privacy:
• Your private keys never leave your device
• All secrets stored encrypted
• Open source - fully auditable code
• No analytics or tracking
• Reproducible builds

Ergo is a resilient, open-source blockchain platform designed for developing efficient, secure, and user-friendly decentralized applications. Built on research-backed principles with advanced cryptographic features like Sigma Protocols.

Minimum Requirements:
• Android 7.0 or higher
